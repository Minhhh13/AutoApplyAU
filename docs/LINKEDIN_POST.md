# LinkedIn launch draft

I built AutoApply AU: an open-source Codex and Claude Code skill for Australian internship, vacationer and graduate applications.

It brings the repeated parts of applying into one reusable workflow:

- Find roles across major Australian boards, specialist/startup platforms, government and university portals, and employer sites.
- Check degree windows, work-rights requirements, academic scales and deadlines.
- Prepare tailored CVs and answers from your confirmed experience.
- Fill supported application forms and track progress locally.

You choose whether it stops at review or submits specific applications you authorise. It also preserves uncertain submission outcomes so an interrupted session does not become an accidental duplicate.

This is an early release. The local helpers are tested, and live form filling depends on your agent's browser tools. I'd love feedback from people trying it on Australian programs.

Inspired by Thang Phung's AutoInternshipClaude, adapted for the Australian market.

Code, setup instructions and downloads: https://github.com/Minhhh13/AutoApplyAU
